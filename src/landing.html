Why tsquare

Boards, not single screens

Several devices side by side (phone, tablet, desktop), with sticky notes for open questions. A whole feature on one image.

Written by AI, measured

Sonnet wrote valid tsquare on the first try in 20 of 20 tests. Mistakes come back by line, with "did you mean", so models fix their own output. See the evals.

Ready for your docs

Clean grayscale SVG or PNG with one accent color. Image URLs work anywhere an image does: Notion, GitHub, docs sites.
